Adjustable text size, sessions that come back after a shutdown, and a Resume that always works.

## New

- **Text size**: Ctrl+= (or +) makes the text in all terminals larger, Ctrl+- smaller, Ctrl+0 normal again; Ctrl+mouse wheel over a tile does the same (Cmd on macOS). The size is kept across restarts.
- **Shorter folders**: a tile shows a folder in your home folder from `~` (e.g. `~\projects\shop`); its tooltip has the full path.

## Fixed

- Resume on an ended session's card sometimes did nothing: where the session had left its cursor, the click went to the terminal.
- Sessions that Windows ended while shutting down, restarting or logging off showed as ended after the next start. Now they resume like the others.
- Without Claude Code, reframe now says how to get it, and on Windows that one installed only in WSL is not supported yet.

## Install

You need [Claude Code](https://claude.com/claude-code) installed and logged in. The builds are not code-signed yet:

- **Windows**: `reframe-Setup-0.4.1.exe` installs for your user, without admin rights; installed copies update themselves. If SmartScreen warns, choose _More info_ → _Run anyway_.
- **macOS** (Apple silicon): open the `.dmg` and drag reframe into Applications; the first time, right-click it → _Open_.
- **Linux**: the `.AppImage` (make it executable; it updates itself) or the `.deb`.
